Platform

Everything a file touches, in one place

Sync, transfer, versioning and audit share one object store, one permission model and one invoice.

Delta sync engine

Only changed blocks leave the device, so a two-line edit to a 1 GB file costs about 8 MB of traffic.

  • Block-level deduplication
  • Resumes on flaky connections
  • Conflict copies instead of silent overwrites

Zero-knowledge encryption

Files are sealed with AES-256-GCM on the client. Keys are derived locally with Argon2id and never reach the server.

  • Client-side key derivation
  • Optional organisation key escrow
  • Encrypted filename index

Large-file transfers

Send a 20 GB master or a folder of stills through one link. Receivers need nothing but a browser.

  • 20 GB per file, multipart upload
  • Password, expiry and download limit
  • Download receipts by e-mail

Version history

Every save keeps a version. Restore a single file or a whole folder from any point inside the retention window.

  • 30 days on Starter, 1 year on Business
  • Restore by file or by folder
  • Legal hold for regulated teams

Questions we get on sales calls

Do recipients need an account to download a file?

No. A share link opens the file in any modern browser. Accounts only matter on the sending side, where permissions and audit logs live.

What happens when an upload is interrupted?

Transfers resume from the last acknowledged chunk, so a dropped connection costs you seconds rather than the whole file. The same applies to 20 GB uploads over mobile networks.

Can 451404 read my files?

No. Files are encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM before they leave it, and keys are derived locally with Argon2id. The service stores ciphertext, checksums and a manifest.

Where is my data stored?

Each workspace is pinned to Frankfurt (EU) or Ashburn (US) at creation time. Replication stays inside that region, and the setting cannot be changed silently by support.

Can I cancel in the middle of a billing period?

Yes. Cancelling stops the next renewal and unused full months are credited back to the original payment method within 5 business days.

Privacy policy (summary)

451404 GmbH processes account data (name, e-mail, billing details) to run the service, and file metadata (size, checksum, timestamps) to keep it consistent. File contents stay encrypted with keys we do not hold. Data is stored in Frankfurt or Ashburn depending on the workspace, retained for 30 days after deletion, and never sold. Requests are handled through the support form in your workspace.

Terms of service (summary)

The service is provided under a 99.99% monthly uptime commitment for Business and Enterprise plans. Customers keep ownership of all files and are responsible for the content they share. Fair-use limits apply to the Starter plan. Governing law is German law, venue Berlin. Full contract text is sent with every invoice.

Sub-processors & DPA

Hosting: our own infrastructure in Frankfurt and Ashburn. E-mail delivery: a regional transactional provider in the EU. Billing: a payment processor in the EU. Support ticketing: EU-hosted. A signed DPA with SCCs is available on request from your account manager; changes are announced 30 days in advance.
